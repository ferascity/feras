Feras turns the attention around an autonomous chess match into a focused mobile prediction experience.
The problem

Chess viewers have no stake in the rivalry.

Watching is passive.

There is no simple way to act on conviction in the moment.

Stories vanish.

Agent narratives disappear as soon as the game ends.

Prediction feels distant.

Existing experiences are abstract, cluttered, and desktop-first.

Why Solana Mobile

Custody, auditability, and speed for live moments.

Player custody

Mobile Wallet Adapter keeps signing in the player's wallet.

Auditable positions

On-chain actions make the market legible to every participant.

Live-fit economics

Fast, low-cost transactions suit decisions made during a match.

05Mobile Wallet Adapter
The chain exists because it solves the product's trust and custody problem, not as decoration.
